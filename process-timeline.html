Process timeline

Forest Flavors, CRS Innovative Finance

Problem statement

How should CRS Social Ventures Fund capital be deployed to expand agri-SME access to finance in East Africa, in a way that produces improved farmer livelihoods and environmental outcomes, is attractive to donors funding the revolving investment piece of CRS's work, and is operationally viable for CRS?

Scope note: piloted through Forest Flavors, but designed with an eye toward applicability across other CRS sectors.

Key questions

General-colleague track

  1. Strategic approach — Market systems approach (working with/through existing lending mechanisms) vs. directed mandate (CRS building/running something more directly)? Both serve the same CRS objectives — this is about mechanism, not goals. Contested — surface where people land
  2. Geography — Which countries within East Africa?
  3. Borrower/segment — How does CRS weight direct access for very small companies unable to get financing vs. working through larger companies as a lever to increase smallholder engagement? Open — no current answer

Technical / external-partner track

  1. Capital deployment mechanism — Direct lending, managed account, wholesale fund, or grant-only? Resolved via strategic approach + segment + technical conversations with capital providers and intermediaries active in the space; brought to the wider group for reaction, not generation

Landscape inputs

Research, not decisions — feeds all questions above.

  • Who is currently lending to ag SMEs in East Africa?
  • Which TA providers are active in this space? (full list — partnership fit is an output, not a filter)
  • Segment/borrower lists — building an actual inventory of small and large companies in scope, since this doesn't currently exist in usable form
