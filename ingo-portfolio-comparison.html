Follow-up note

Mercy Corps Ventures vs. Mercy Corps AgriFin — these are separate initiatives under the same parent organization, not two versions of the same thing. Mercy Corps AgriFin (MCAF) is a donor-funded facilitation program (Gates Foundation, Mastercard Foundation, Swiss Development Corporation) that helps partners — banks, mobile network operators, agribusinesses — design and test digital financial services for smallholder farmers. It makes no direct investments. Mercy Corps Ventures (MCV), profiled above, is the actual capital vehicle. The two are linked only administratively (MCV's Scott Onder sits on AgriFin's advisory council) and share a parent org and target geography.
