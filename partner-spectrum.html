Where partners sit: control to leverage

Forest Flavors, CRS Innovative Finance

This page maps the range of relationships CRS could have with a partner in a lending facility, using a narrower definition of control than the one on control-and-sequencing.html: who holds decision rights over individual lending decisions, not branding or reputational ownership.

This page maps the range of relationships CRS could have with a partner in a lending facility, from direct control on one end to pure leverage on the other. control-and-sequencing.html defined control by branding and reputational ownership — on that basis, CRS's stake in Isidro and Azure is total, since both are unmistakably CRS-branded regardless of who holds the investment committee vote.

This page uses a narrower, more literal definition of control: who holds decision rights over individual lending decisions. On that basis, Mandate and CRS-sponsored facilities are essentially equivalent — both carry full CRS branding and pipeline control. The only reason Mandate sits to the left here is that Isidro's and Azure's investment committees don't give CRS a majority vote, while a mandate arrangement has no investment committee at all. The one lens that meaningfully splits Mandate from Isidro and Azure — who runs day-to-day operations — isn't itself a control factor; it's a separate decision about capacity, not authority.
