Reading the market before the deal

Segmentation

Segmentation establishes the core constraint. Donors usually segment by value chain, but if you're a lender, the most useful variable, the one that tells you the most for the least effort, is the loan size a business is looking for.

Banks vs. funds

Content coming soon. Banks are profitable, funds are not. Funds are easier to work with but have higher cost structures and dollar-denominated funding. Banks have cheaper local currency funding through deposits but are harder to work with. Ecosystem-building work points toward banks; niche or pilot work points toward funds.

Pipeline

How many companies you need to see before you close a loan depends heavily on how that pipeline gets built.

Click anywhere, or press space, to advance

Two funnels, one for an investment-led approach and one for a technical-assistance-led approach, that fill with dots representing companies and narrow from screening to evaluation to closed loans, with commentary above narrating each stage before switching from one funnel to the other

An average fund screens around 300 companies to build its pipeline. Of those, about 50 get evaluated in real depth. And roughly 10 actually close as loans. Now compare that to sourcing pipeline through an existing technical assistance activity.

A conservative estimate is that it takes twice as many companies to start — around 600. About 100 of those get evaluated in depth. And the same 10 close as loans — twice the screening effort for the same result.
