Reading the market before the deal

Segmentation

Segmentation establishes the core constraint. Donors usually segment by value chain, but if you're a lender, the most useful variable, the one that tells you the most for the least effort, is the loan size a business is looking for.

Banks vs. funds

Content coming soon. Banks are profitable, funds are not. Funds are easier to work with but have higher cost structures and dollar-denominated funding. Banks have cheaper local currency funding through deposits but are harder to work with. Ecosystem-building work points toward banks; niche or pilot work points toward funds.
